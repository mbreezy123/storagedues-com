FAQ

Straight answers

Who are you?

I'm Matt Breen, the founder. Storage Dues is a small, new, independent service that I run myself, from New York. You deal with me directly.

What does it cost?

Free for your first batch, then simple flat pricing quoted upfront before you commit. No card needed to start.

What do you need from me?

The standard report export from storEDGE or SiteLink (rent roll, payment and messaging reports). Nothing else, and no logins required.

Do you contact my customers?

No. Storage Dues never contacts tenants. You decide what to do with each line on the board.

How do I opt out?

Reply "no" to any email from me and I won't email you again. You can also ask me to delete anything you've sent by writing to matt@storagedues.com, and I will.
